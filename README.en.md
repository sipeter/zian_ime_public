# ZianIME

[简体中文](README.md) · [User guide](docs/USAGE.md) · [Privacy policy](https://sipeter.github.io/zian_ime_public/privacy.html) · [Issues](https://github.com/sipeter/zian_ime_public/issues)

ZianIME is an Android voice keyboard that combines on-device speech recognition with Chinese and English keyboards. Speak or type to enter text. Once the speech model is downloaded, recognition works offline on your phone.

## Features

- On-device speech recognition with live text preview.
- Chinese Pinyin, English, number and symbol keyboards.
- Optional local recognition history with search, export and deletion; disabled by default.
- Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean interfaces. Interface and recognition language are separate settings.
- No account required.

## Getting started

Supports **Android 8.0 or later on 64-bit ARM devices**. Recognition performance depends on your device.

1. After installation, enable ZianIME in Android's keyboard settings and select it as your keyboard.
2. Download the speech model in the app settings. Wi-Fi is recommended.
3. Grant microphone permission when using voice input.

See the [user guide](docs/USAGE.md) for detailed steps and troubleshooting.

## Privacy and support

Read the [privacy policy](https://sipeter.github.io/zian_ime_public/privacy.html) and [user guide](docs/USAGE.md). Model downloads use the internet; local recognition does not send recordings to our recognition servers. Optional history is stored in the app's private no-backup directory.

Use [Issues](https://github.com/sipeter/zian_ime_public/issues/new/choose) for reproducible problems and suggestions. Contact **sdrxdev@gmail.com** privately for privacy or security concerns. Do not post private recordings, dictated text, logs, passwords or keys in public issues.
