# ZianIME Privacy Policy


Updated: November 16, 2026


This policy applies to **ZianIME**, the Android voice keyboard (**com.zian.ime**) provided by **ZianStudio**. Privacy contact: **sdrxdev@gmail.com**.


## 1. Voice and keyboard input


The microphone is used when you activate voice input. Speech recognition runs on your phone after the recognition model is available. The app does not send your recordings, typed content or recognition results to our servers for recognition, advertising or analytics. As a keyboard, it processes input and the editing context provided by the current app to enter text. Text you submit is then handled by the destination app under its own privacy practices.


## 2. Model downloads and internet access


Downloading the speech model requires an HTTPS connection to **dl.ziansoft.com**. The download service and its network providers necessarily receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested file, and may process technical access logs to deliver and protect the service. Model download requests do not include your voice recordings or typed text. Local recognition can work without this connection once the model is downloaded. This is not a promise that the app never uses the internet.


## 3. Optional text history


Saving recognized text is **off by default**. If you enable it, completed speech recognition results are stored privately on your device. The app excludes fields identified by Android as password fields and fields requesting no personalized learning; this depends on the destination app marking its fields correctly. You can browse, search, delete or export records. The optional automatic cleanup retains the latest 30 calendar days; when it is off, records remain until you delete them or clear the app data. Turning history off stops new records but does not delete existing ones.


History is stored in Android's no-backup directory. If you export records, the chosen file or storage provider is outside this private history store; deleting the in-app record does not delete an exported copy.


## 4. Local storage, backups and deletion


The app stores settings, downloaded models and local keyboard dictionary data to provide its features. You can remove app-managed data through Android's app storage controls or uninstall the app. Android backup or device transfer may include eligible settings and other app data according to your system settings and device provider; the private recognition-history directory is excluded from automatic backup. Copies you export and backups managed by your device provider must be managed separately.


## 5. Permissions and third parties


Microphone permission enables speech input and may be revoked in Android settings. Internet permission supports model downloads. The app must be enabled as an input method to function as a keyboard. This app does not require an accessibility service or PC connection. It contains no advertising SDK or app-level analytics SDK and does not require an account. Google Play and Android may separately process installation, diagnostic or backup data under their own policies and your settings.


## 6. Contact and policy updates


If you contact **sdrxdev@gmail.com**, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please do not include private recordings or input history unless needed for your request. You may contact us with privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its update date and made available with the app or on our website.




# ZianIME 语音输入法隐私政策


更新日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日


本政策适用于 **ZianStudio** 提供的 **ZianIME 语音输入法（com.zian.ime）**。隐私联系邮箱：**sdrxdev@gmail.com**。


## 1. 语音与键盘输入


您启动语音输入时，应用使用麦克风采集语音。在识别模型就绪后，语音识别在手机本地完成。应用不会将录音、键盘输入内容或识别结果发送至我们的服务器用于识别、广告或分析。作为输入法，应用处理输入和当前应用提供的编辑上下文，以完成文字输入。您提交给其他应用的文字由目标应用按照其自身隐私规则处理。


## 2. 模型下载与联网


下载语音模型需要通过 HTTPS 连接 **dl.ziansoft.com**。下载服务及其网络服务商会接收完成连接所必需的信息，例如 IP 地址和所请求的文件，并可能处理用于提供和保护服务的技术访问日志。模型下载请求不包含您的录音或输入文字。模型下载完成后，本地识别无需保持该网络连接。这不代表应用从不访问互联网。


## 3. 可选文字日志


保存识别文字**默认关闭**。开启后，完成的语音识别结果保存在设备内的应用私有目录。应用排除被 Android 标识为密码的输入框及声明禁止个性化学习的输入框；这依赖目标应用正确标识输入框。您可以查看、搜索、删除或导出日志。可选自动清理功能保留最近 30 个自然日；未开启时，记录将保留至您删除记录或清除应用数据。关闭日志开关只停止新增记录，不会删除已有记录。


日志存放在 Android 的不备份目录。主动导出的文件属于您选择的文件或存储服务，不再属于该私有日志目录；删除应用内记录不会删除导出副本。


## 4. 本地存储、备份和删除


应用在本地保存设置、下载的模型和键盘词库数据，以提供相应功能。您可通过 Android 的应用存储管理或卸载应用移除应用管理的数据。根据系统设置和设备提供方的行为，Android 备份或设备迁移可能包含符合条件的设置及其他应用数据；私有识别日志目录不参与自动备份。导出文件和设备提供方管理的备份需要单独管理。


## 5. 权限与第三方


麦克风权限用于语音输入，您可随时在 Android 设置中撤销。网络权限用于模型下载。使用键盘需要在系统中启用本输入法。本应用不需要无障碍服务或电脑连接，不包含广告 SDK 或应用级统计分析 SDK，也不要求注册账号。Google Play 和 Android 可能依照其政策及您的设置，独立处理安装、诊断或备份数据。


## 6. 联系与政策更新


当您联系 **sdrxdev@gmail.com** 时，您的邮箱地址及主动提供的信息用于回复您的请求。除非解决问题确有需要，请勿发送私人录音或输入日志。您可通过此邮箱咨询隐私问题或申请删除客服往来信息；适用的记录保存要求除外。本政策发生变更时，会更新日期，并通过应用或官网提供更新内容。
